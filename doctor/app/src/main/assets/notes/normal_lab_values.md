# Clinical Reference Laboratory Values

Standard adult reference intervals. Values may vary slightly by analytical laboratory.

---

## 1. Complete Blood Count (CBC)

| Parameter | Male Reference | Female Reference | Unit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hemoglobin (Hb)** | 13.5 – 17.5 | 12.0 – 15.5 | g/dL |
| **Hematocrit (Hct)** | 41 – 50 | 36 – 44 | % |
| **RBC Count** | 4.5 – 5.9 | 4.0 – 5.2 | $\times 10^6/\mu\text{L}$ |
| **MCV** | 80 – 100 | 80 – 100 | fL |
| **MCH** | 27 – 33 | 27 – 33 | pg |
| **MCHC** | 32 – 36 | 32 – 36 | g/dL |
| **Platelets** | 150 – 450 | 150 – 450 | $\times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$ |
| **Total WBC** | 4.0 – 11.0 | 4.0 – 11.0 | $\times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$ |

---

## 2. Basic Metabolic Panel & Electrolytes

| Analyte | Conventional Interval | SI Interval |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sodium ($\text{Na}^+$)** | 135 – 145 mEq/L | 135 – 145 mmol/L |
| **Potassium ($\text{K}^+$)** | 3.5 – 5.0 mEq/L | 3.5 – 5.0 mmol/L |
| **Chloride ($\text{Cl}^-$)** | 96 – 106 mEq/L | 96 – 106 mmol/L |
| **Bicarbonate ($\text{HCO}_3^-$)** | 22 – 29 mEq/L | 22 – 29 mmol/L |
| **Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN)** | 7 – 20 mg/dL | 2.5 – 7.1 mmol/L |
| **Serum Creatinine** | 0.7 – 1.3 mg/dL (M) / 0.6 – 1.1 mg/dL (F) | 62 – 115 $\mu\text{mol/L}$ |
| **Fasting Blood Glucose** | 70 – 99 mg/dL | 3.9 – 5.5 mmol/L |
| **Total Calcium** | 8.5 – 10.5 mg/dL | 2.15 – 2.55 mmol/L |

---

## 3. Arterial Blood Gases (ABG at Sea Level, Room Air)

- **pH**: 7.35 – 7.45
- **$\text{pCO}_2$**: 35 – 45 mmHg
- **$\text{pO}_2$**: 80 – 100 mmHg
- **$\text{HCO}_3^-$**: 22 – 26 mEq/L
- **Base Excess**: −2 to +2 mEq/L
- **$\text{SaO}_2$**: 95% – 100%
