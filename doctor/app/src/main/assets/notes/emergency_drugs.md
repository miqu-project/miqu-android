# Emergency Drug Dosing & Resuscitation Protocols

Rapid bedside reference for acute resuscitation, anaphylaxis, and critical care pharmacotherapy.

---

## 1. Anaphylaxis Management

First-line treatment is **immediate intramuscular Adrenaline (Epinephrine)** in the anterolateral thigh. Do not delay!

| Age / Weight | Adrenaline 1:1000 (1 mg/mL) IM Dose | Volume |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adult (> 12 years / > 50 kg)** | 0.5 mg IM | 0.5 mL |
| **Child (6–12 years / 25–50 kg)** | 0.3 mg IM | 0.3 mL |
| **Child (< 6 years / < 25 kg)** | 0.15 mg IM | 0.15 mL |

- **Repeat**: Every 5 to 15 minutes if symptoms persist or deteriorate.
- **Adjuncts**:
  - High-flow oxygen (10–15 L/min via non-rebreather mask).
  - IV fluid resuscitation (Normal Saline 20 mL/kg bolus in children, 500–1000 mL in adults).
  - Second-line: IV Hydrocortisone (100–200 mg) + IV Chlorpheniramine (10 mg).

---

## 2. Adult Cardiac Arrest (ACLS Algorithm)

- **CPR**: Continuous 30:2 ratio (or continuous chest compressions at 100–120 bpm with advanced airway).
- **Shockable (VF / pVT)**:
  - Shock $\rightarrow$ CPR 2 min $\rightarrow$ Shock $\rightarrow$ CPR 2 min + **Adrenaline 1 mg IV/IO** (repeat every 3–5 min) $\rightarrow$ Shock $\rightarrow$ CPR 2 min + **Amiodarone 300 mg IV/IO** (first dose) or Lidocaine 1–1.5 mg/kg $\rightarrow$ Second Amiodarone dose: 150 mg IV.
- **Non-Shockable (PEA / Asystole)**:
  - CPR immediately.
  - **Adrenaline 1 mg IV/IO** as soon as feasible, repeat every 3–5 min.
  - Identify and treat **reversible causes (5 H's and 5 T's)**:
    - *Hypovolemia, Hypoxia, Hydrogen ion (acidosis), Hypo/Hyperkalemia, Hypothermia*
    - *Tension pneumothorax, Tamponade (cardiac), Toxins, Thrombosis (pulmonary), Thrombosis (coronary)*.

---

## 3. Acute Severe Asthma

- **Inhaled Salbutamol**: 2.5–5 mg via oxygen-driven nebulizer every 20 minutes (or continuous).
- **Inhaled Ipratropium Bromide**: 500 mcg nebulized with Salbutamol.
- **Systemic Corticosteroids**: Oral Prednisolone 40–50 mg or IV Hydrocortisone 100 mg.
- **Severe / Life-Threatening Refractory**:
  - IV Magnesium Sulfate: 1.2–2 g IV infusion over 20 minutes.
