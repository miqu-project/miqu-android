# Obstetric Dating & Clinical Milestones Reference

Accurate gestational age assessment is essential for timing antenatal screening tests, managing preterm labor, and planning delivery.

---

## 1. Pregnancy Dating Formulas

- **Naegele's Rule** (standard 28-day cycle):
  - EDD = First day of LMP + 1 year − 3 months + 7 days (or LMP + 280 days).
  - If cycle length differs from 28 days: Add (Cycle Length − 28) days to the EDD.
- **Ultrasound Dating Discrepancy Rules (ACOG Guidelines)**:
  - $\le 8^{+6}$ weeks: Dating by CRL if discrepancy > 5 days.
  - $9^{+0} - 13^{+6}$ weeks: Dating by CRL if discrepancy > 7 days.
  - $14^{+0} - 15^{+6}$ weeks: BPD/HC/AC/FL if discrepancy > 7 days.
  - $16^{+0} - 21^{+6}$ weeks: Discrepancy > 10 days.

## 2. Trimester Breakdown & Key Clinical Milestones

### First Trimester (0 – 13 Weeks 6 Days)
- **6–8 Weeks**: First viability transvaginal ultrasound (fetal heart rate, gestational sac, yolk sac).
- **11–13⁺⁶ Weeks**:
  - **Nuchal Translucency (NT) Scan** & First Trimester Combined Screening (Free $\beta$-hCG + PAPP-A).
  - Cell-Free Fetal DNA (NIPT) screening for aneuploidy (Trisomy 21, 18, 13).

### Second Trimester (14 – 27 Weeks 6 Days)
- **16–18 Weeks**: Quadruple Marker Screen (if first trimester screening missed).
- **18–22 Weeks**: Detailed Target / Level II **Fetal Anomaly Scan** (TIFFA) and cervical length assessment.
- **24–28 Weeks**:
  - 75g Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT) for Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM).
  - Anti-D antibody titer screen for Rh-negative mothers (RhIG 300 mcg at 28 weeks).

### Third Trimester (28 Weeks – Delivery)
- **28–32 Weeks**: Fetal growth ultrasound and umbilical artery Doppler.
- **35–37 Weeks**: **Group B Streptococcus (GBS)** rectovaginal culture.
- **$\ge 40$ Weeks**: Post-term surveillance (biophysical profile, non-stress test, amniotic fluid index).

---

## 3. Red-Flag Antenatal Symptoms
Immediate clinical evaluation required if:
1. Vaginal bleeding (abruptio placentae / placenta previa).
2. Sudden severe headache, visual disturbances, epigastric pain (Preeclampsia / HELLP).
3. Decreased fetal movements after 28 weeks.
4. Sudden gush or continuous leaking of clear fluid (PPROM / PROM).
5. Regular painful uterine contractions before 37 weeks.
